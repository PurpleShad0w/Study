For the course of Intermediate Statistics, we were given a data-set focused on a specific Massive Open Online Course. This data-set includes personal information on the courses' participants, various information about how they learned about the course or the difficulties they had with it, as well as data on whether they answered certain quizzes, their grades and if they passed the course in the end or not. We firstly had to gather the different csv files containing the data and merge them altogether, while making sure to create a way to identify which data came from which file later on using a new variable. We also had to make sure there was no duplicate students left by merging various rows in order to obtain a clean data-set. \\ \\
